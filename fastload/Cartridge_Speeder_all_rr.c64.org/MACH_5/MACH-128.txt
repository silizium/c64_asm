For creating the eprom contents for a real "MACH 128" cartridge eprom
you will have to concatenate a "MACH 5" and a "MACH 128"- Under linux
e.g.

cat MACH_5*.bin MACH_128.bin > MACH_128_full.bin

will work.
It does not make much sense to join these binaries for emulator use
esp. since C128 cartridge emulation is not supported by VICE so far.

MACH-5 binaries coming with an additional "_128" suffix are suitable
for C128 use as they have an extra function especially:
"TOGGLE C128 KEYS"
